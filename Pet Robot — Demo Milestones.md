# Pet Robot — Demo Milestones & Artifacts

**Project:** Pet Robot — a robot designed to interact with and entertain dogs
**Flagship Project for:** Georgia Tech + Engineering Admits, Class of 2027
**Lane:** Mech/CAD (design for manufacturability, mechanical systems)
**Story:** "I build robots that help the people and animals around me."

---

## Project Spec (Summary)

**Problem:** A neighbor's dog lost its companion. Existing robot dog toys move aimlessly — no real interaction.

**Approach:** AI-powered 4-wheeled robot that identifies and engages with dogs. Core mechanical design: rack-and-pinion front steering, rear-wheel drive (1-2 HD Hex motors), 4 axles, structural extrusion chassis.

**Full success criteria** (finished product — not required for early demos):
- Detects obstacles and avoids them
- Autonomously navigates an apartment
- Identifies dogs vs. people and other pets
- Interacts with the dog meaningfully
- Durable enough for real use
