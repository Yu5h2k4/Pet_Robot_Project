## Problem
* One of my neighbor's dogs has passed away, the other dog is saddened by their loss, so I thought of a robot that will play with him.
* There are already robot toys for dogs, but they only wiggle and move aimlessly, I want to make one that will interact with the neighbor's dog. 

## Approach
* An AI powered 4-wheeled robot that prompts a dog to chase it. Later add other functionalities that allows the robot to interact with the dog in multiple ways.
	* Utilizes 2 cameras, front & back, to identify pets and obstacles, with assistance of additional sensors
	* 4 wheels, with 1-2 motors to power the rear motors.
	* 1 Servo motor to check directions using rack and pinion for the front 2 wheels.

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Can detect obstacles and avoids them
- [ ] Can autonomously drive around an apartment
- [ ] Can identify dogs apart from people, objects, and other pets
- [ ] Can interact with the dog
- [ ] Can endure the interactions with the dog
